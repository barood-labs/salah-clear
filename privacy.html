Privacy Policy — Salah Clear

Effective: 19 October 2026 · Applies to the Salah Clear website/PWA and the Salah Clear Android app.

  • No data collection. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking SDKs.
  • Location. If you allow it, your device location is used only on your device to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. It is never sent to us or anyone else. You can instead choose a city manually.
  • Notifications and settings are stored locally on your device and removed when you uninstall the app or clear site data.
  • Purchases. The optional supporter unlock is processed by Google Play (Android) or Payhip (web). We receive only what those services provide to confirm the purchase; see their privacy policies.
  • Children. The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.
  • Changes. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
